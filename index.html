What is UnstableThoughts.blog
UnstableThoughts is an independent writing platform focused on political philosophy, Indian politics, development, and social issues. It values slow thinking, honest doubt, and perspectives that evolve rather than remain fixed.
Who writes the articles?
Everything here is written by a single author [Abin]. If you'd like to contribute, feel free to reach out at: unstablethoughtsindia@gmail.com
How often do you publish?
New pieces are published roughly three times a week. The focus is on quality over quantity-each article goes through several drafts before it’s published. Subscribe to the newsletter to get every new piece delivered straight to your inbox.
Our goal?
To create a meaningful impact through honest and thoughtful writing. Advice to readers: Read with an open mind and form your own understanding of the truth.

Stay in the loop.

One to three article a week. Thoughtful, clear, and always free.
